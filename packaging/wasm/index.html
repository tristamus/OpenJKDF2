      Features What this web version adds

      Works in both Jedi Knight and Mysteries of the Sith.

      Play online

      • Host or join from the game's Multiplayer menu.
      • Browsers connect directly, with the lobby as a fallback.
      • No install, port forwarding or account.

      Custom maps and mods

      • Missing the host's maps or mods? They download automatically as you join.
      • Mix and match mods from your game folder.

      • Settings, characters, maps, mods and your mod picks are kept too.

      Getting Started

      1. Install Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II on this computer, from Steam or GOG for example. In Steam, right-click the game, then Manage, Browse local files to find it.
      2. Click Play JKDF2 or a room, then pick the game folder: the one with episode and resource inside. Each room asks for its own game's folder.
      3. To play in a window, untick Play in fullscreen before you start. This browser remembers it.
      4. In the game, choose Multiplayer to host or join a match.
      5. Mods go in your game folder: in mod, mods, a folder of their own, or as a GOB in Resource. GOBs, zips, unzipped mods and JK Patch Commander patches all work. Players who join you get your mods, and you get the host's when you join.

      Your Files

      • Your game stays on your computer. This tab reads your game folder. Nothing is uploaded to our server.
      • Except custom maps and mods you host. Players who join without them get a copy from your browser, directly or through our server, which doesn't keep it. Original game files are never sent: each player has their own.
      • Size limits. A host can send a map of up to 1 GB and up to 1 GB of mods. Past that, players need their own copy. Zipped mods open if they unzip to 2 GB or less. This browser keeps up to 2 GB of maps and 2 GB of mods from hosts, dropping the least recently used.
      • Why we ask for the folder. This site has no game files, so it runs from your copy. If your browser says "upload", that's just its word for reading a folder.
      • You only pick the folder once. This browser saves the files the game needs: about 250 MB for Jedi Knight, 500 MB for Mysteries of the Sith, plus mods. Added mods or maps? Pick the folder again. To save nothing, untick "Save the game files in this browser".
      • Your characters, controls and screenshots are saved in this browser too. Clearing this site's data deletes them all, and the saved game files. Download the screenshots you want to keep.
      • Matches are recorded. When a match in the rooms ends, its scoreboard, who killed whom, the level and the mod go on a page anyone can open, with the names players used in the game, and it counts in the standings and on each player's own page. To be in them as Anonymous, untick "Put my name on match pages" under Leaderboard or when you start. To keep your matches together, this browser gets a random player number when you play, kept here and in a cookie. There are no accounts, so a browser without its number is taken for the player whose name it plays under.
      • Played at the old address? If the game then asks for your folder, pick it once more.

      Your Screenshots

      Press F8 or F12 in a game (or your screenshot key) to save a picture here, in this browser. In Edge, use F8.

        Game Help

        • Use a current Chrome or Firefox on a computer. Phones and tablets also work if you have the game files on them, and have a keyboard and mouse attached, and are able to upload them from your device... =]
        • Files missing? Pick the folder with episode and resource directly inside, not one above or below it.
        • Won't start, or only a few frames per second? Turn on graphics acceleration in your browser's settings and restart it. This page shows how when it's off.

        Choose the folder where Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II (or Mysteries of the Sith) is installed, the one with episode and resource inside.

        • Your game stays on your computer. It's read in this tab, not uploaded. Only custom maps and mods you host go to players who lack them. Original game files are never sent.

        If your browser says "upload", that's just its word for letting this page read the folder. Choose Upload to continue.

        Mods to play with
        Saved from online games

        Mods start as you left them, and new ones start ticked. When two mods change the same file, the higher one wins. Players who join get your mods (never original game files), and if you join a game with different mods, the game restarts with those. Graphics packs (JK HD Models, JKE, JKR) are never sent: everyone keeps their own, in every game.

        Brave doesn't let the game keep Esc, Ctrl+W, Ctrl+T or Ctrl+N in fullscreen: Esc leaves fullscreen, and Ctrl+W closes the tab. If you fire with Ctrl, give Fire another key in the game's Setup > Controls.

        Pick your game folder and this browser checks it for new ones at every start. If the game is in Program Files, pick a folder outside it instead, with maps in an Episode folder and mods at its top.
