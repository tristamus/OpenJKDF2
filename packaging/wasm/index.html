Getting Started

  1. You need Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II installed on this computer, for example from Steam or GOG. In Steam, right-click the game, then choose Manage, then Browse local files to see where it is.
  2. Click Play JKDF2 or any room, then pick the game's folder: the one with the episode and resource folders inside.
  3. When the game starts, choose Multiplayer to host a match or join one.

Your Files

  • Nothing is uploaded. When you pick your game folder, this page reads the files on your computer and loads them into the game in this browser tab. They're never sent to our server or to other players.
  • Why we ask for the folder. This web version comes with no game files at all, so it only runs from a copy of the game you already have. The check makes sure the folder holds the game's files before it starts.
  • About the browser's "upload" message. Your browser may ask whether to "upload" the files. That's its standard wording for letting a page read a folder. The files still stay on your computer.
  • Your characters and controls are saved in this browser, so they're there next time.

Game Help

  • Use a current Chrome or Firefox on a computer. Phones and tablets can't open a game folder.
  • If the page says files are missing, you picked a folder one level too high or too low. Pick the one that has episode and resource directly inside.
  • The game needs WebGL 2. If it won't start, turn on hardware acceleration in your browser's settings.
